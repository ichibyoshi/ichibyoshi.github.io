What is Kendo?

Kendo (剣道), or "way of the sword", is the Japanese martial art of sword-fighting. Kendo developed from traditional techniques of Japanese swordsmanship known as kenjutsu and now is also an international sport.

Who are we?

The club was founded in 2006 and has offered training at all levels ever since. Friendly and open, we welcome all ages and abilities. Senior instructor John Bates has been practising since 2003 and won Gold in national Kata competition.

Ichi ByōShi Class Details

Ichi ByōShi Kendo Club key details:

  • Seniors classes in Aylesbury
  • Thursday 19:30-21:30: Aylesbury High School
  • Junior classes in Aylesbury
  • Saturday 10:00-12:00: Aylesbury High School
  • We cater for beginners and experienced Kendoka
  • Classes are not gender specific

Ichi ByōShi trains to the highest standard, drawing on coaching from leading Kendo instructors in the UK and Japan. Senior BKA instructors visit the club, and we travel together to seminars led by senior Japanese teachers.

We focus on developing our members both as kendoka and as people. Respect and discipline are central to our training, but so is enjoying it. Every student learns all aspects of Kendo, and those who want to compete are supported to do so.
