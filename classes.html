Starting out

Beginners are welcome at either session, and juniors start on Saturdays. Your first session is free, and you don’t need any kit to begin.

  1. 1 Get in touch Send us a message so we know to expect you. Contact us
  2. 2 Get insured Take out three months’ temporary membership with the BKA. How
  3. 3 Come for a free session Wear loose sports clothes and bring a water bottle. We train barefoot, and we lend you a shinai for your first sessions.
  4. 4 Join the club If you want to carry on, membership is £45 a month by standing order. New starters spend around six weeks on the basics before joining the main class.

Will I get hit?

Not until you have your own armour. We start with the traditional kata forms and basic techniques.

Do I need to know any Japanese?

No. You pick it up as you train, and our glossary is there if you want a head start.

Is it self-defence?

Not directly, but it teaches you to read an opponent, and to judge distance, speed and timing.

Can I watch first?

Yes. You are welcome to observe a session, though you can’t train on that visit.

FOR PARENTS AND CARERS

Kendo for young people

Young people aged 8 and over are welcome at our Saturday session, where they train alongside adult members. Kendo builds focus, confidence and respect, and juniors learn the same art in the same way as everyone else.

No special equipment is needed for around the first four months.

Fees and BKA membership

Club membership is £45 a month by standing order, the same for everyone and whichever session you attend.

You must also be insured to train, and the only insurance we accept comes with membership of the British Kendo Association. New starters can take three months’ temporary membership, then convert to full membership. Joining from another club? Bring proof of your membership.
